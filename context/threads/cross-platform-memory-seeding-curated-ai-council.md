## Decisions and rationale

### Accepted design decisions

1. **Separate stable context from platform behavior.** The Core Payload should be reusable across platforms, while each activation prompt explains the platform’s council role and asks it to confirm what it retained.
2. **Use differentiated council roles.** The council is not five copies of one assistant. The design gives synthesis, documentation, established-context merging, Microsoft 365 integration, and independent multimodal/long-context judgment distinct responsibilities.
3. **Transfer in dependency order.** The proposed order is Perplexity, Notion, ChatGPT, Copilot, then Gemini. This is a source proposal, not an independently verified requirement.
4. **Compare retention after transfer.** The Council Roll Call is the acceptance test for cross-platform consistency and exposes where reinforcement is needed.
5. **Treat the context stack as layered.** Instant memory is for high-frequency orientation; Notion is for structured briefs and extracts; GitHub is for versioned source artifacts; a semantic archive is for deeper retrieval. A raw export remains an archive, not automatically usable memory.
6. **Curate rather than dump.** The human orchestrator is responsible for deciding what becomes durable context, what stays archived, and what must remain outside the AI stack.
7. **Keep the repository artifact redacted.** The durable local extract carries the method and operating value without copying private personal, employer-confidential, account, or third-party material.

### Alternatives and tradeoffs

- **One universal mega-prompt only:** easy to copy, but it loses platform differentiation and encourages prompt entanglement.
- **Separate bespoke profiles with no shared core:** better local calibration, but creates drift and makes cross-platform reconciliation difficult.
- **Raw-export ingestion as “memory”:** preserves everything but harms retrieval quality, privacy boundaries, and portability. The source favors curated layers with on-demand archive access.
- **Notion-only canonicalization:** improves structure and search, but creates vendor dependence and does not replace versioned repository artifacts.

## Reusable methods and assets

### Memory-seeding protocol

1. Build a curated Core Payload from durable identity, work, preferences, philosophy, and project context.
2. Assign each target platform a specific council role and a role-relevant activation prompt.
3. Ask the platform to confirm retained material, identify storage limits or gaps, and record a dated transfer anchor where supported.
4. Run one common Council Roll Call across all platforms.
5. Reinforce weak areas and reseed after major changes.

### Core Payload design guidance

- Keep identity and preferences factual, compact, and revisable.
- Separate public professional context from employer-confidential material.
- Treat project lists as status-bearing context, not permanent truth.
- Prefer named frameworks, decision rules, and examples over vague personality claims.
- State what the AI should do when uncertain: cite, ask at most one useful question, label assumptions, and avoid invented facts.
