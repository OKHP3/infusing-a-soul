## Physical Canon

- Mechanical terror bird, not an eagle, not a crow. "Something that looked like it would win a fight with a server rack."
- Hooked beak, compact folded wings, dark patinated armor (brown to black, green seams, blue mineral traces), heavy clawed talons, glowing orange eyes.
- Perches on, or stands beside, a retro CRT with visible wiring and a classic keyboard. Blueprint grid; horizontal stripes in carbon green, rust orange, faded mustard, dark plum, slate teal.
- Left shoulder: one plate fitted backward, removed, fitted again. It never matched. It works.

## Story Canon (three eras)

1. **The Maker (Bronze Age Mesopotamia).** Commissioned as a flying eagle. The Maker found fossil terror-bird bones and built a creature whose weight "had somewhere to go." It broke a hammer at its demonstration. In the foundry it found flaws before they were visible and dismantled a magnificent shield with weak hidden seams. It "learned to watch the cloth." Sunk in a cistern; the water kept it.
2. **The Mechanic (1853 to 1873).** Looted, shipped to England, repaired openly with iron braces and brass bushings. The Mechanic refused to hide repairs behind fake ancient plates. It moved only on borrowed power. It pulled a rivet from a boiler fitting that had just passed inspection. The name MurderBird first appeared in an expense book beside a replacement vise. Six mind assemblies failed. Final note: "The missing part is not a gear."
3. **The Builder (2025).** Added a heart (sealed power core, return threshold it can measure) and a mind (learning lattice behind the eyes). The mind learned to accept the twice-fitted plate: a deviation "could be damage, but it could also be history or a workable compromise." First choice: shifted weight before a support failed. First question on the CRT: "What am I for?" Answer: "Find what doesn't hold." It pulled a loose bracket. "That was temporary." Temporary described the intention, not the strength.

Closing image: the bird watches the operator's hands, knows the pause before a task is abandoned and "the phrase that means a weakness will be left for later." A talon touches the casing. The file opens again.

## Doctrine Canon (manifesto)

- "The MurderBird is not cute. It is the part of the forge that watches the output and asks whether the system actually held."
- "The bird does not sit. It occupies." Grip is "not aggression for its own sake. That is how the work gets done."
- "The first answer is ore, not steel."
- "Useful is not final. Persuasive is not honest. Shiny is not structurally sound."
- "No. Not yet. That is not overkill. That is care with its sleeves rolled up."
- "OverKill Hill P3 is not anti-simple. It is anti-premature-simple."
- "Words are upstream architecture. If the words are vague, the system inherits the fog."
- "AI is a power tool, not a priest." "The council beats the oracle."
- "Diagrams must pay rent." "The first diagram is just a lie with arrows."
- "Precision keeps the work sharp. Protocol keeps the work repeatable. Promptcraft keeps the work human enough to matter."
- Seed phrase: "If something is worth doing, it is worth overdoing." Battle cry: "Yeah, but what if we built it better?"
